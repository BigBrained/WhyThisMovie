Behind the picks

How the recommender works

  • Data: the 13 genre-split CSV files in dataset/, deduplicated by IMDb ID.
  • Content-based: your ratings build a taste profile from genre overlap and film attributes.
  • Film similarity: genre, director, cast and release era help identify related movies.
  • Quality signal: IMDb rating and vote count help distinguish otherwise similar candidates.
  • Explanations: each reason is grounded in the ratings and shared genres used by the model, rather than generated free-form text.
  • Feedback: “Less like this” hides a film and lowers the weight of its genres. “More like this” raises those genre weights.

Privacy and limitations

  • Recommendation calculations run locally in your browser. The app does not upload your ratings or save them between sessions.
  • The CSVs provide catalogue metadata and aggregate IMDb rating/vote counts, but not per-user rating histories, so recommendations are content-based rather than collaborative. The benchmark is unavailable for this source.
  • The app uses posters/MOVIE_INDEX.jpg when a local poster exists. Missing posters use the built-in SVG generator. Movie indexes are zero-based, so the first movie uses posters/0.jpg.
